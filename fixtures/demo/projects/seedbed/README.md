# Seedbed

A garden planner that knows your frost dates. Draw your beds, pick what to grow, and Seedbed tells you when to sow, plant out and harvest.

> [!WARNING]
> Blocked on the weather API's pricing for frost forecasts. The alerts wait until they reply.

## What works

- [x] Beds drawn to scale, with the hours of sun each one gets
- [x] A sowing calendar for 140 vegetables and herbs
- [ ] Frost alerts
- [ ] Offline map tiles for the allotment

## Planting rules

| Crop | Sow indoors | Plant out | Spacing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tomato | 8 weeks before the last frost | After the last frost | 45 cm |
| Courgette | 4 weeks before | 2 weeks after | 90 cm |
| Broad bean | — | 6 weeks before | 20 cm |
| Basil | 6 weeks before | 3 weeks after | 25 cm |
