Call with the weather API's sales team, 24 September

Frost forecasts are in their agriculture tier, not the standard one.
The standard tier has daily lows, but only five days ahead.
Agriculture adds the risk of ground frost, ten days ahead, by postcode.

They charge per request, with the first 10,000 a month free.
One request per garden per day is about 3,000 a day once the beta opens,
well past the free allowance.

What we talked through:
- Cache forecasts by postcode, not by garden. Most allotments share one.
- Check twice a day, not every hour. The forecast barely moves.
- Ask for their small-business rate. They'll send it by Friday.

If nothing arrives by Friday, the planting rules ship without alerts,
as the weekly review says.
