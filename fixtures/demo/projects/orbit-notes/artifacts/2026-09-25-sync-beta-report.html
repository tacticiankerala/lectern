Sync beta: the first week

Orbit Notes · 21 to 25 September

Offline sync went to twelve testers on Monday. Every edit synced through the change log, no note lost text, and catching up stayed well under half a second, even after two weeks offline.

Conflicts

Each of the six was one note edited on two devices while both were offline. Every one kept both versions, side by side, as the plan says it should. Two testers thought the second copy was a bug.
