# Roast tomato galette

A free-form tart for the end of summer, when there are more tomatoes than plans for them. The pastry forgives rough handling.

## Pastry

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Plain flour | 200 g |
| Cold butter, cubed | 120 g |
| Salt | a pinch |
| Iced water | 3 to 4 tbsp |

Rub the butter into the flour until the largest pieces are the size of peas. Add just enough water to bring it together, flatten into a disc and chill for 30 minutes.

## Filling

1. Slice 600 g of tomatoes, salt them lightly and leave them on a rack for 20 minutes.
2. Roll the pastry into a rough circle, about 3 mm thick.
3. Spread 2 tbsp of mustard over the middle, leaving a wide border.
4. Layer the tomatoes, scatter thyme and grated cheese, and fold the border over the edge.
5. Brush the pastry with egg and bake at 200 °C for 40 minutes, until deep golden.

> [!TIP]
> Pat the tomatoes dry before they go in, or the base turns soft.
