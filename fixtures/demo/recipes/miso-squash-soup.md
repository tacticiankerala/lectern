# Miso squash soup

Sweet, savoury and thick enough to eat with a fork. It travels well in a flask.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Butternut squash, peeled and cubed | 1.5 kg |
| Onions, sliced | 2 |
| Ginger, grated | a thumb |
| White miso | 3 tbsp |
| Vegetable stock | 1.5 l |

## Method

1. Soften the onions slowly in a little oil, about 15 minutes.
2. Add the ginger and the squash, and stir for a couple of minutes.
3. Pour in the stock and simmer until the squash falls apart, about 25 minutes.
4. Take the pan off the heat, whisk the miso with a ladle of the soup, then stir it back in.
5. Blend until smooth, and season to taste.

Miso loses its flavour if it boils, so add it last and reheat gently.
