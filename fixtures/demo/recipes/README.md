# Friday lunch rota

Whoever demos on Friday cooks. Everything here feeds eight and survives being carried across town.

| Week | Dish | Goes with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [[sourdough-focaccia\|Sourdough focaccia]] | A green salad |
| 2 | [[roast-tomato-galette\|Roast tomato galette]] | Whatever cheese is left |
| 3 | [[miso-squash-soup\|Miso squash soup]] | Last week's bread, toasted |
| 4 | Whatever the garden gave us | — |

- [x] Nothing that needs a knife at the table
