# Sourdough focaccia

Crisp underneath, soft inside, and forgiving about timing. Mix it on Thursday evening for Friday's lunch.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Baker's % |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Bread flour | 500 g | 100% |
| Water | 400 g | 80% |
| Active starter | 100 g | 20% |
| Salt | 10 g | 2% |
| Olive oil | 60 g, and more for the tin | — |

## Method

1. Mix the flour, water and starter until no dry flour is left. Rest for 30 minutes.
2. Add the salt with a splash of water and squeeze it through the dough.
3. Over the next two hours, do four sets of stretch and folds, 30 minutes apart.
4. Cover and refrigerate overnight, for 12 to 18 hours.
5. Tip into an oiled tin and leave somewhere warm until puffy and jiggly, about 3 hours.
6. Dimple with oiled fingers, scatter rosemary and flaky salt, and bake at 230 °C for 25 minutes.

> Let it cool for ten minutes before cutting. It's hard to wait, but the crumb sets as it cools.

## Variations

- Cherry tomatoes and olives, pressed in before baking
- Thin slices of potato, with rosemary and a little garlic
- Red onion, slowly softened first
